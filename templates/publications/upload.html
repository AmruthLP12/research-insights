1 Export Checklist

Follow these steps in Web of Science to generate a compatible file:

  1. 1
    Search & Select

    Run your query in Web of Science or open your Marked List.

  2. 2
    Export Format

    Click Export and select Tab delimited file (or Tab-delimited (Win)). Do not select Excel (.xls) or BibTeX.

  3. 3
    Record Content

    Choose Full Record (recommended) or Full Record and Cited References.

  4. 4
    Record Range

    Set record range to at most 500 records per batch (e.g., Records 1 to 500) and click Export.

Troubleshooting & Limits

  • • Oversized Files: If your export exceeds 5 MB or 500 records, split your export into smaller annual or departmental ranges (e.g. 1–500, 501–1000).
  • • Wrong Format: Files must contain standard WoS field tags (such as TI, SO, AF). CSV, Excel, PDF, or raw text files will be rejected.
